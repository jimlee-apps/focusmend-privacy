Where does FocusMend store my data?

Only on your Mac. Focus sessions, tasks and habits are in ~/Library/Application Support/FocusMend. Settings are in ~/Library/Preferences/com.focusmend.app.plist. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else.

How do I delete all my data?

  1. In FocusMend, choose Setup > Reset Data > Reset All History. Or, to remove everything:
  2. Quit FocusMend and delete the app.
  3. Delete ~/Library/Application Support/FocusMend and ~/Library/Preferences/com.focusmend.app.plist.
  4. If you used an earlier version (Baud), also delete ~/Library/Application Support/Baud and ~/Library/Preferences/com.baud.app.plist.

Does FocusMend need an account or collect analytics?

No. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

How do I report a bug or ask a question?
