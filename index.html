Privacy Policy: FocusMend

Effective date: November 5, 2026
Developer: Jim Lee, Taiwan
Contact: jim120791185@gmail.com

The short version

FocusMend doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you create in FocusMend stays on your Mac.

What FocusMend stores, and where

To work, FocusMend saves the following only on your Mac. On the Mac, the app's identifier is com.focusmend.app.

In its data folder, ~/Library/Application Support/FocusMend:

  • Focus sessions: when they start and stop, which mode, and how long you focused.
  • Tasks and habits: the names you type, how they repeat, their focus length, and when you checked them off.

In its settings file, ~/Library/Preferences/com.focusmend.app.plist (separate from the data folder):

  • Settings: e.g. your daily target, workday hours, theme (System, Light or Dark), menu bar options and clock format.
  • Display name: used for the greeting (e.g. "Good work, Alex"). By default FocusMend uses the first word of your Mac account's full name. You can change it in Setup. It never leaves your Mac.

FocusMend doesn't send any of this to me or to anyone else. I can't see it.

What FocusMend doesn't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No reading of other apps, windows, websites, files, location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • FocusMend listens only for your Mac going to sleep or waking up, so a running timer stays accurate. That information isn't stored or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

  • In FocusMend: Setup > Reset Data > Reset All History deletes every saved focus session. Reset Today clears today's sessions and any running timer. Delete individual tasks on the Tasks tab.
  • To remove everything, quit FocusMend, delete the app, and delete its data folder ~/Library/Application Support/FocusMend, plus its settings file ~/Library/Preferences/com.focusmend.app.plist.
  • If you used an earlier version of the app under its previous name, Baud, FocusMend copied your data on first launch and left the old copies in place. For a full wipe, also delete ~/Library/Application Support/Baud and ~/Library/Preferences/com.baud.app.plist.
  • Your own Mac backups (e.g. Time Machine) may contain a copy of FocusMend's data, as with any app. That copy is under your control.

Crash reports

If you've turned on System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > Share with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash and performance reports for FocusMend with me. I use them only to fix bugs. You can turn this off any time in that setting.

If you email me

If you contact me for support, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you. I don't add you to any mailing list. I delete support emails once your issue is solved.

Children

FocusMend doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If FocusMend ever starts collecting data, I'll say so here and on the download page first.
